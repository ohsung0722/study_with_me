## 3. Brighter corridor
The corridor reads dull/dark. Make it clearly brighter and inviting in both day and night:
- Lighter floor and walls (raise the corridor tints; a lighter runner rug), ceiling light panels and/or wall sconces with emissive shades + fake glow pools (glow decals like the lamp glow), and a corridor entry in the per-zone lamp fill so its walls are lit.
- At night the corridor should be warmly lit (not lofi-dark like the reading room).
- **Real PointLights stay ≤ 6** (light pool). New lights are emissive + glow only, or pool candidates.
